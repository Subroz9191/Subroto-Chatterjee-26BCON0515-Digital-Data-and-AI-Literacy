My Engineering Journey

My journey into technology began with an innate curiosity about how things operate beneath the surface. Ever since I was young, I couldn't resist opening up electronics and dissecting systems to ask: “How does this work, and how can we make it faster, cleaner, and smarter?”

Entering my First Year of B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, that curiosity turned into dedicated engineering discipline. I fell in love with low-level systems programming in C, unraveling pointers and direct memory allocation, and the structural beauty of Java for object-oriented software design.

Beyond traditional software, my heart is captivated by Fintech—the intersection where financial mechanisms, algorithmic efficiency, and distributed ledgers meet. I believe that engineering trustworthy, high-speed digital financial rails will empower the next generation of global economic inclusion.
